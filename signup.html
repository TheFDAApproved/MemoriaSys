Terms and Conditions

Section 01

Accuracy of Cemetery Records

As an authorized user of this system, you bear the strict responsibility of ensuring data integrity. Plot mapping updates, deceased identities, interments, and ownership deed links must be double-verified before database commitment. Erroneous data entry directly compromises physical ground layout operations.

Section 02

Transaction Records and Accountability

Every modification, deletion, financial entry, or deed transfer executed under your credentials will generate a permanent system audit log linked directly to your profile. Falsifying administrative details, transaction dates, or financial logs will trigger an immediate administrative inquiry and suspension of system clearance.

Section 03

Confidentiality of Family Data

The personal information, financial payments, and contact addresses of deed holders stored in this system are strictly confidential. Staff members are expressly forbidden from downloading, exporting, or printing customer profiles for unauthorized use or distribution outside official office administrative pipelines.

Section 04

Restricted Access & Security Hygiene

Accounts are personal and strictly non-transferable. Staff members must never share system credentials or perform actions on behalf of another office colleague. Terminals must be logged out or locked immediately when leaving an office desk to prevent unauthorized database access by third parties.

Section 05

Modifications to Terms

The administration reserves the right to update, alter, or modify these administrative policies and system protocols at any time without prior notice. Continued utilization of your system credentials following any updates constitutes definitive acceptance of the revised operational mandates.
